One image that sums up the world news of **Tuesday 17 November 2026**, drawn as a newspaper front page: Ukraine's strike collapsing a span of the Crimea (Kerch) Bridge and Russia's answer on Kyiv's bridges; President Trump landing in Manila for the ASEAN and Quad summits on his way to talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin; tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz as a third U.S. carrier arrives; ULA's Vulcan rocket failure destroying 40 Amazon Leo satellites; and GTA VI arriving in two days — plus a ticker with four more of the day's stories. The picture was composed programmatically (Python + Pillow) so every headline is crisp and readable at thumbnail size.

